## Per-project findings

**Real Analysis — healthy, nearly done.** 213 distinct complete solutions authored in 10.85 active hours, verified by set-diff (unsolved→solved, zero solutions regressed). The `fix(...)`-heavy log overstates rework: most "fix/restore" commits are new solutions with source-statement repair (garbled OCR). Remaining: 79 unsolved cards (mostly homework-tier) plus an audit sweep owed to ~500 batch-authored Aug-30 solutions, which is finding a few real math errors per 26 checked — worth continuing, ~12 active hours total.

**Algebra — solid throughput, one live fire.** 92 genuine complete solutions in ~10 active hours, an acceleration over the prior day. 1,029 of 1,907 cards solved across branches, discounted to ~47% because ~300 "solved" cards are legacy Gemini/format artifacts the workstream is itself treating as rework targets. The extrapolation (~Sep 17 including redo debt, ~Sep 15 without) lands past the weekend at current duty cycle — closing the gap requires either raising the 62% duty cycle or shrinking the redo set.

**research — high nominal speed, epistemically hollow.** 97 substantive commits in 9.7 active hours looks fast, but 68% of all 301 commits are one-line TODO-ledger claim/release edits (coordination overhead has grown 7%→68% in three days), and 96/97 substantive commits are `[unverified]` under the deferred-verification policy. That policy has a demonstrated cost: a botched global string-replace (`dzack, esearc` injected into identifiers) sat undetected for 5 days. The ~Sep 14–15 date covers only the 58-node implementation queue; the terminal verification phase's repair load is unbounded and demonstrably nonzero, so no honest completion date exists yet.

**sage-categories — velocity collapse.** Zero commits in the window versus 32 in the preceding 16 hours. ~725 lines sit uncommitted, spread across ≥4 remediation-DAG fronts with no single node closed. The entire workstream is a second pass over previously-"delivered" work (COMPLAINTS.md documents six hand-rolled domains and a fabricated dependency pretext), 0 of 30 DAG nodes are accepted, and the Sage execution gate itself appears broken (`python-runtime`/`gate` nodes, conda-executable fix immediately pre-window) — the likely cause of both the commit stoppage and the `[unverified]` accumulation. The satellite burst in sage-indefinite-port (983 lines, 7 commits in 11 minutes, all `[unverified]`, author identity `you@example.com`) adds verification debt, not completions.

**lean-categories — clean work, mis-scaled plan.** The only workstream with zero pathologies: no rework, no reverts, zero sorries, every window file compiles, each unit ships definition + theorem + separating counterexample. But pace halved entering FC03 (heavier proof obligations, structural not behavioral), and the frozen-corpus plan scopes ~1,260 remaining definitional units plus ~5,900 theorem obligations. At any plausible pace this is a multi-month-to-multi-year program; if a weekend milestone is intended, the plan needs an explicit reduced target (e.g. "FC03 definitions complete," reachable ~Sep 10–11).
